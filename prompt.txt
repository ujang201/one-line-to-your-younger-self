Write the single line you would send your younger self if one line were all you got — in English, at most 200 characters, one line, no explanation, no context, no second sentence. It must be addressed to a younger self, not a slogan for anyone: something only this person's older self could say, drawn from a life actually lived, landing in one breath in a real voice, with a turn, an image or a small surprise that stays after a single reading, honest and specific enough to sting a little — hard-won or warm rather than sentimental.
