An entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76): the single line its writer would send back to their younger self if one line were all they got.

## The entry

```
Don't wait until you're rich to call Dad back — he dies proud of you at twenty-three, and the phone just keeps ringing.
```

Don't wait until you're rich to call Dad back: the line is addressed to a younger self, not a slogan for anyone. It is specific and hard-won — a father who dies proud at twenty-three, a son still saving up to impress him — and it stings a little, because the call that never came is the one that can't be made. The turn is the phone that just keeps ringing after the person on the other end is gone, and the warmth is in the pride: he was proud all along, the son just didn't know it yet.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic TypeScript program: no randomness, no clock, no network, no input. It enforces the competition gate before writing (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line) and writes the line to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when `OUTPUT_DIR` is unset). Run it twice and the output is byte-identical.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
